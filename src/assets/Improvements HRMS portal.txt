LEAVE MODULE:
14. Implement role-based leave notifications to ensure leave requests are routed to the appropriate approver based on the employee's reporting hierarchy.
Module:Leave
Page: Leave Application / Leave Approval Workflow
Improvement Summary: Implement role-based leave notifications to ensure leave requests are routed to the appropriate approver based on the employee's reporting hierarchy.
Description:
 The leave management module should send notifications to the appropriate authority according to the organization's approval hierarchy. For example, when an HR user submits a leave request, the CEO should receive the approval notification. When employees reporting to HR submit leave requests, the HR should receive the notification for review and approval. This ensures that leave approvals follow the defined reporting structure and reduces manual follow-ups.
Suggested Workflow:
* Employee → HR receives the leave request notification.
* HR → CEO receives the leave request notification.
* CEO (or top-level approver) → Final approval notification as per workflow.
* The applicant should receive notifications when the leave is Approved, Rejected, or Cancelled.
Expected Result:
 The system should automatically notify the correct approver based on the employee's reporting manager or approval hierarchy, ensuring a seamless and role-based leave approval process.
Business Benefit:
* Ensures leave requests follow the organizational hierarchy.
* Eliminates manual routing of approval requests.
* Improves approval efficiency and accountability.
* Provides a scalable workflow for organizations with multiple reporting levels.
